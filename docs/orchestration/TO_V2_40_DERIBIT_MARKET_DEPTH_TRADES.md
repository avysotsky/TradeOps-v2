## Functional scope
- New independent public-data controller under apps/TradeOps.TerminalApi/Controllers/DeribitMarketController.cs. Four authenticated local GET-only routes provide real Deribit market instrument discovery, orderbook depth, public last trades and ticker. All market requests use the fixed www.deribit.com or test.deribit.com HTTPS origin and fixed public JSON-RPC methods. No exchange credential headers, trading operations, PostgreSQL or workers.
- Instruments: currency BTC/ETH and kind future/option with search, max 150 returned per request, a 60-second cache per host+currency+kind, inactive filtering, and minimum 1.1-second spacing for uncached instrument lists. Deribit instruments API has a specific ~1 request/second rate limit.
- Book: top 10 native bid/ask levels [price, amount], no conversion to USD or coins. Public trades: last 15 executed market trades with direction, price, amount and time. Market ticker supports scoped BTC/ETH futures and options.
- React displays instrument browser, book, public trade tape, and 8-second polling. A held position can be clicked to switch only the market-data instrument. Options price shows up to 6 fractional digits. Market failures do not prevent private account/positions from displaying. Separate trading ticket remains limited to BTC-PERPETUAL and ETH-PERPETUAL and its mainnet execution flags remain forcibly false in owner launcher.
- The existing one-command Windows DPAPI read-only mainnet launcher now pins this new branch. No operator passphrase entry; the ephemeral local operator key is generated by the launcher and only passed to the localhost API and server-side Vite proxy for GETs.
